# Moderation and Trust & Safety

## Goals

- allow honest negative and positive experience;
- reduce spam, harassment, doxxing, fabricated campaigns and illegal content;
- preserve due process for authors and affected parties;
- avoid letting owners, advertisers or developers buy suppression.

## Content policy baseline

Disallow or redact:

- personal identity numbers, document numbers, private phone/email/address details;
- exact apartment numbers unless necessary and safely restricted;
- threats, harassment and hate content;
- faces or private conversations without appropriate consent;
- accusations stated as proven criminal facts without appropriate context/evidence;
- irrelevant disputes with identifiable private persons;
- copied reviews, spam, paid manipulation and coordinated brigading;
- content unrelated to the property/living experience;
- instructions facilitating unlawful access or harm.

Allow:

- first-hand opinions and clearly framed experiences;
- criticism of service, construction, utilities and management;
- factual claims with dates/context;
- photographs of relevant conditions after privacy redaction;
- representative replies that follow the same rules.

## Moderation strategy

### Before publication

Use for:

- new/untrusted accounts;
- evidence-backed allegations with high legal/privacy risk;
- content flagged by automated detectors;
- private media or possible personal data;
- coordinated submission bursts.

### After publication

May be enabled later for trusted contributors and low-risk updates, with rapid reporting and rollback.

## Decision actions

- approve;
- approve with redaction;
- request changes;
- reject;
- temporarily hide pending review;
- remove;
- restrict account;
- escalate to legal/security review.

Every action requires a reason code. User-visible explanations should be specific enough to correct the issue without exposing internal abuse signals.

## Reports

Report categories:

- personal data/doxxing;
- false or misleading claim;
- harassment/threat;
- conflict of interest/paid review;
- not about this property;
- duplicate/spam;
- copyright/media issue;
- outdated/resolved issue;
- other.

A “false claim” report does not automatically remove content. Assess whether it is opinion, first-hand experience, a verifiable factual allegation, or clearly fabricated/manipulated content.

## Appeals

- preserve original decision and policy version;
- permit one structured appeal with new context;
- use a different reviewer for serious cases when possible;
- record outcome and explanation;
- measure overturned decisions.

## Right of reply

Verified representatives may reply publicly. Rules:

- reply is labelled;
- no access to reviewer’s private identity/evidence;
- no threats, pressure or off-platform contact requests;
- no score change;
- reply versions and moderation history preserved;
- author may mark whether an issue was resolved.

## Anti-capture rules

- commercial customers cannot directly access moderation decisions;
- sponsored status is isolated from review ranking;
- takedown requests from owners/developers use the same case workflow;
- emergency legal requests have a documented escalation path;
- moderators disclose conflicts and can recuse.
