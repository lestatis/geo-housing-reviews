# Content Dispute Runbook

## Intake

Collect target URL/ID, complainant relationship, specific disputed statements, reason category, requested action and supporting context. Do not promise removal.

## Triage

1. Is there immediate risk: doxxing, threat, private document, unsafe location disclosure?
2. Is the content opinion, first-hand experience, or a factual allegation?
3. Is a narrow redaction sufficient?
4. Is there evidence of manipulation or conflict of interest?
5. Does the author need a request for clarification/evidence?
6. Does legal review need to be involved?

## Actions

- no action with explanation;
- annotate/update context;
- redact personal data;
- request author changes;
- temporarily restrict visibility;
- remove under policy;
- offer representative reply;
- escalate.

## Record

Store policy version, decision reason, evidence references, reviewer, timestamps, user-visible notice and appeal result. Never expose reviewer identity or raw verification evidence to the complainant.
